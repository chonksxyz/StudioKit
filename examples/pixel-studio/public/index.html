How it works

  • Shift-click a painted cell to select it — in any tool, Shift selects rather than erases. Bare canvas has nothing to pick up.
  • Shift-drag to sweep a run of cells in, or shift-drag from a selected cell to sweep them back out.
  • Let go of Shift — the selection stays. Drag any selected cell to move the whole shape.
  • Dropping commits the move as one undo step and ends the selection, so the next stroke draws normally.
  • The arrow keys nudge a selection too; those land when you draw, select something else, or press ↵.
  • With cells selected, clicking a swatch recolors them — the selection stays, so try a few.
  • Del or ⌫ empties the selected cells and clears the selection — one undo step.
  • Moved pixels overwrite what they land on, and anything pushed off the canvas is deleted. Cancel before it lands to put everything back.
