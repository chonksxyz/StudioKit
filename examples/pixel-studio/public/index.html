Nothing selected.

How it works

  • Shift-click cells to add them to a selection; shift-click a selected cell to drop it.
  • Shift-drag to sweep a run of cells in or out in one gesture.
  • Let go of Shift — the selection stays. Drag any selected cell to move the whole shape, or nudge it with the arrow keys.
  • Moved pixels overwrite what they land on. Anything pushed off the canvas is deleted on apply.
  • Apply writes the move as one undo step; Cancel puts everything back.
